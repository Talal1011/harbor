# <:harbor:1528569473659834459> Harbor Update

**Hey @everyone!**
[Beta] v0.9.125 is being prepared. <:hooray:1528571878866948258>

**What's included:**

**Playback, subtitles, and X-Ray**

> - Arabic/English subtitle labels and grouping fixes, with manual Live Try timing preserved through seeking and pause/resume
> - Encrypted HLS playback improvements, Windows audio-output recovery, and fullscreen window restoration
> - Select an X-Ray actor to browse their movies and TV shows without leaving the player
> - Linux desktop media controls for playback, seeking, volume, and metadata

**Continue Watching and your library**

> - Dismissed cards return when you make further progress, with Trakt/Simkl refresh improvements and shared-profile safeguards
> - Optional episode-still artwork for Continue Watching, with your saved frame as a fallback
> - Season selection follows watch progress
> - Drag-and-drop list reordering and descriptions for custom and featured lists

**Navigation, appearance, and more**

> - TV/controller navigation, settings focus, home search, and Arabic/RTL improvements
> - Restored standard row arrows and ElegantFin sidebar/back alignment fixes
> - Refreshed content advisory popup and studio/network facts
> - Custom background and dimming for the “Who's watching” screen
> - Full Edge TTS voice discovery for ebooks, manga improvements, desktop notifications, and controller cursor options
> - Experimental changelogs remain available after installation, including offline

**Account changes**

> - Discord account sign-in, linking, and recovery have been removed. Discord Rich Presence remains available.

**A note from Talal**
